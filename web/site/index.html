FAQ

Questions, answered

Still unsure? Open the terminal and try the free plan, it takes a minute.

Do I need to know ICT already?

It helps. ICT Terminal draws and names the concepts on the chart, so it is also a good way to learn them, but it does not replace learning how and when to use them.

Which markets are covered?

Today: gold (XAUUSD), silver (XAGUSD), Nasdaq 100, S&P 500, Bitcoin, EURUSD and GBPUSD as CFDs. More symbols and brokers' feeds are being added.

Does it trade for me?

Only if you install the MT5 bridge on your own MetaTrader 5 account and switch it on. Auto-trading is limited to models the ICT Terminal team has approved after testing, and to the plans that include it. Start on a demo account.

Are the signals guaranteed to win?

No. Setups follow fixed rules; many will lose. Past results do not promise future results. Please read the risk disclaimer.

How do I pay?

Inside the terminal, under your account. The available payment methods are shown there.
