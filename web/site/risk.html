Risk disclaimer

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Trading contracts for difference (CFDs), forex, indices, metals and cryptocurrencies carries a high level of risk. Because of leverage, you can lose money quickly, and you can lose all of the money you deposit with your broker.

Not financial advice

ICT Terminal is charting and analysis software. Its indicators, model setups, assistant answers and any other content are information only. They are not investment advice, a recommendation to buy or sell, or an offer of any financial service. Decide every trade yourself and, if needed, ask a licensed financial adviser.

No promise of results

Setups are found by fixed rules based on ICT concepts. Many setups lose. Backtests and past results, where shown, are not a reliable guide to future results. Spreads, slippage, swaps, news and broker conditions change outcomes.

Automatic trading

If you connect the ICT Bridge EA to your MetaTrader account, it places orders on your account using the limits you set. Software, internet, VPS, broker or data problems can cause missed, late or wrong orders. Test on a demo account first, use risk limits you can afford, and watch your account. You are responsible for every trade on it.

Only risk what you can afford to lose

Do not trade with borrowed money or money you need. If you are not sure you understand the risks, do not trade.
