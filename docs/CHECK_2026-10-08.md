`/api/v1/oauth/<provider>/start` answers "This login method is not available right now" (404).

`FACEBOOK_APP_SECRET` in the VPS `admin_panel/.env` (plus the Settings switches, on by default).

`https://ictapi.iccterminal.trade/api/v1/oauth/google/callback` and `.../oauth/facebook/callback`.
